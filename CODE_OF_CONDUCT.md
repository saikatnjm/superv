# Code of Conduct

SuperV follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/saikatnjm). Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove comments or block people who break these rules.
